## Objective

Explore a small-to-medium business network topology in Cisco Packet Tracer and identify:

- End devices
- Intermediate devices
- Network media
- LANs and WANs
- Client-server model concepts

## Topics Covered

- Physical and logical network components
- End devices vs intermediate devices
- Network media
- LAN and WAN concepts
- Internet connectivity
- Client-server architecture

## Activities

- Identify network devices in the topology
- Differentiate LANs and WANs
- Examine network connections
- Explore Packet Tracer network representation

## Key Takeaways

- Intermediate devices connect and manage network traffic.
- End devices are the source and destination of data.
- LANs connect devices within a limited area.
- WANs interconnect multiple LANs across large distances.
- The Internet is a global network of interconnected networks.
